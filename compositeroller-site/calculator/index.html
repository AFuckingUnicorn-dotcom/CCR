Steel and composite tube weights (kg/m) are Composite Roller Co.'s own engineer-verified figures, matched by diameter and duty class (light duty: composite 15mm wall / steel ~3.5mm wall, bearings 6205–6307; heavy duty: composite 20mm+ wall / steel 6mm wall, bearings 6308+). These cover the tube/shell only — actual assembled roller weight also includes bearing housing, bearing, seal, and shaft, which this estimate does not add. Where a diameter's data has a caveat (e.g. a non-standard wall thickness on file), it's noted next to that selection. Steel market value defaults to a recent scrap steel commodity benchmark (~$0.42/kg, i.e. ~$420/metric ton, Trading Economics, early April 2026) and is fully editable — it's an illustrative reference for the value of the weight removed, not a resale, trade-in, or product cost figure, and commodity prices move frequently and vary by region and grade. Noise reduction (≥70%) and power/friction reduction (8% minimum per compositeroller.com, or 12–26% per CCR spec sheet, model-dependent) are separate published figures, not derived from the tube weight data. Actual results vary by belt speed, load, environment, and roller specification. This calculator provides a directional estimate only — contact our engineers for a formal assessment specific to your site.
